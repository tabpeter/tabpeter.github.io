My work in Colombia was made possible by a grant from the [Fulbright Study/Research program](https://us.fulbrightonline.org/applicants/types-of-awards/study-research) and the Colombian institution [ICETEX](https://web.icetex.gov.co/portal). 

In May 2025, I graduated with my PhD in Biostatistics from the [University of Iowa](https://www.public-health.uiowa.edu/biostat/).
